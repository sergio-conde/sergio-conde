### Hi, I'm Sergio

**Neuroscientist and engineer.** I turn brain recordings and behavior into
something we can understand.

Postdoctoral researcher in the
[Willuhn lab](https://nin.nl/research-groups/willuhn/) (Neuromodulation & Behavior)
at the [Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience](https://nin.nl/) and the
Department of Psychiatry, Amsterdam UMC. I study how cortical activity integrates
sensory and cognitive information to guide behavior: how the collective activity
of neuronal populations represents sensory and behavioral features, and what
changes in compulsive behavior. I do this through multidimensional analysis of
electrophysiological and optical imaging recordings in rodents.

Electronic engineer by training: I started with human EEG during my master's,
moved to animal models (cats and rodents) during my PhD in neuroscience, and
have been writing analysis code along the way.

- **I work with:** MATLAB (Signal Processing, Statistics and Machine Learning,
  and Deep Learning toolboxes; FieldTrip) · Git/GitHub · Python and SQL *(currently learning)*
- **I analyze:** electrophysiology (LFPs, single units), calcium imaging, operant behavior
- **Methods:**
  - Electrophysiology preprocessing: artifact removal, filtering, re-referencing
  - Spectral analysis and LFP–LFP coherence
  - Spiking analysis: spike sorting, spike–spike and spike–field synchrony
  - Population dynamics (neural assembly analysis, dPCA, neural trajectories) and decoding
- **Interests:** scientific programming, data visualization, scientific
  communication, reproducible research

### Featured work

**Electrophysiological markers of compulsive behavior** · *code private until publication*  
Analysis pipeline for simultaneous LFP and tetrode recordings from the
**orbitofrontal cortex and striatum** of rats in the schedule-induced polydipsia
(SIP) model of compulsivity.
- Behavior: parsing of Med-PC sessions into trials, licking bouts and water intake
- LFP: preprocessing, power spectra and spectrograms, cross-frequency modulation, early-vs-late session comparisons (ANOVA)
- Single units: spike sorting and cell-type classification (pyramidal/interneuron, MSN/FSI)
- OFC–striatum synchrony: LFP–LFP coherence, spike–spike and spike–field synchrony

**[calcium_rats_gonogo](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/calcium_rats_gonogo)**  
Single-cell miniscope calcium imaging in the **prelimbic and infralimbic cortex**
during a Go/No-Go task: responsive-cell detection, population trajectories,
dPCA and decoding of task variables.

**[behavior-medpc](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/behavior-medpc)**  
Toolbox to turn raw Med-PC (Med Associates) files into trial-by-trial behavioral
data: trials, events, latencies, bouts and histograms. Used across lab projects.

**[matlab-utilities](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/matlab-utilities)**  
Shared MATLAB helpers for project file management and plotting.
