### Featured work

**Electrophysiological markers of compulsive behavior** · *code private until publication*
Analysis pipeline for simultaneous LFP and tetrode recordings from the
**orbitofrontal cortex and striatum** of rats in the schedule-induced polydipsia
(SIP) model of compulsivity.
- Behavior: parsing of Med-PC sessions into trials, licking bouts and water intake
- LFP: preprocessing, power spectra and spectrograms, early-vs-late session comparisons (ANOVA)
- Single units: spike sorting and cell-type classification (pyramidal/interneuron, MSN/FSI)
- OFC–striatum synchrony: LFP–LFP coherence, spike–spike and spike–field synchrony

**[calcium_rats_gonogo](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/calcium_rats_gonogo)**
Single-cell miniscope calcium imaging in the **prelimbic and infralimbic cortex**
during a Go/No-Go task: responsive-cell detection, population trajectories,
dPCA and decoding of task variables.

**[medpc-behavior](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/medpc-behavior)**
Toolbox to turn raw Med-PC (Med Associates) files into trial-by-trial behavioral
data: trials, events, latencies, bouts and histograms. Used across lab projects.

**[matlab-utilities](https://github.com/Willuhn-Group/matlab-utilities)**
Shared MATLAB helpers for project file management and plotting.
